REFERENCE // FIELD QUESTIONS

What is a tradeable coupon?

A coupon is an event reward that may be activated for the vehicle or listed on the market when trade rules allow it.

What does time-gate mean?

A time-gate is a stage that becomes available only after a scheduled reset. Plan sessions around the window, not just the total points.

GJN, GE and Silver Lions: what changes?

GJN is market currency, GE is premium currency, and Silver Lions pay ordinary game costs. They have separate uses.

Do event vehicles return?

Some vehicles can return through later sales or market listings, but there is no guaranteed schedule. Treat a live event as the safest acquisition window.

How accurate are the drop rates?

Use the published in-game panel as the authority. This guide is a planning reference and does not promise a specific result.
